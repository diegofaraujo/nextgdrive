Privacy policy

Effective October 25, 2026.

Next GDrive is an open-source desktop application for Linux that keeps your Google Drive on your computer. It runs entirely on your computer and talks directly to Google's servers. There is no Next GDrive server: the developers do not receive, collect, store or have access to any of your data. The source code, which shows exactly what the application does, is public at github.com/diegofaraujo/nextgdrive.

Google user data the application accesses

When you link a Google account, you grant Next GDrive the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive permission ("See, edit, create, and delete all of your Google Drive files"). With it, the application accesses:

  • Your Google Drive files and folders, in My Drive and in the shared drives you belong to: their content and their metadata (names, folders, sizes, modification times, checksums, sharing capabilities), including items in the Drive trash.
  • Your account's name, email address and storage quota, to show which account is linked and how much space is used and available.

How the application uses that data

Only to provide the features you use, on your own computer:

  • copying the folders you choose to your computer and keeping them in sync in both directions (uploading, downloading, renaming, moving and trashing items as you change them on either side);
  • showing your Drive as a folder and downloading files when you open them, including files you mark as available offline;
  • listing and restoring items from your Google Drive trash;
  • showing your storage usage, the sync status and errors.

Next GDrive does not use your data for advertising, analytics, profiling, training machine-learning models, or any purpose other than those features. It does not sell, transfer or share your data with anyone. It contains no telemetry, ads or third-party tracking.

Where the data is stored

Only on your computer, in your user account:

  • the files you sync or keep offline, in the folder you choose (by default ~/GoogleDrive);
  • a local record of synced items (names, identifiers, checksums, times), used to tell what changed, in ~/.local/share/nextgdrive;
  • a cache of recently opened streamed files, in ~/.cache/nextgdrive, limited in size;
  • the list of linked accounts (email and name) and your settings, in ~/.config/nextgdrive;
  • the sign-in credential Google issues (a refresh token), only in your system's keyring (a Secret Service provider such as GNOME Keyring or KeePassXC), never in plain files. Short-lived access tokens are kept in memory only.

When installed as a Flatpak, the same data lives under ~/.var/app/io.github.diegofaraujo.NextGDrive/, and the credential in the keyring provided by the system's portal.

The application connects only to Google (accounts.google.com, oauth2.googleapis.com and www.googleapis.com), over encrypted HTTPS connections.

Removing your data

  • Unlinking an account in the application deletes its credential from the keyring and the account from the settings. Files already on your computer are kept, so you do not lose them; you can delete them, and the folders listed above, at any time.
  • Revoking access: you can withdraw Next GDrive's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Uninstalling the application does not delete your files in Google Drive.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Next GDrive's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Children

Next GDrive is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

Changes are published on this page, with a new effective date, and recorded in the project's public history at GitHub.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/diegofaraujo/nextgdrive/issues. To report a security problem privately, use GitHub's security advisories.
